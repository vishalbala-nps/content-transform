# Heat Action Plan 2027: directions to all departments

Office of the Chief Secretary, Government of Vindhara. Memo No. GAD/HAP/2026/118. Dated 3 September 2026.

To: All Heads of Department, District Collectors and Municipal Commissioners.

## Background

The 2026 summer was the most severe on record in the State. The State Weather Office recorded 46 days above 42°C in the capital district, against a ten-year average of 19 days. Hospitals reported 212 deaths from heatstroke between March and June 2026, and 7,340 hospital admissions for heat-related illness.

A review by the State Disaster Management Authority found that cooling centres opened late, outdoor workers continued working through the hottest hours, and several primary health centres ran out of oral rehydration salts in May.

## Directions

Every department is directed to comply with the following ahead of summer 2027.

- Each department and district shall designate a Heat Action nodal officer and report the name to the State Disaster Management Authority by 15 October 2026.
- Municipal bodies shall open at least one cooling centre in every ward with a population above 20,000, operational by 31 March 2027.
- No government department or contractor shall schedule outdoor manual work between 12:00 and 16:00 on any day the State Weather Office issues an orange or red heat alert.
- Every primary health centre shall hold a minimum stock of 2,000 oral rehydration salt sachets from 1 March 2027.
- Every district hospital shall reserve 10 beds for heatstroke cases from 1 April to 30 June 2027.
- District Collectors shall report heat-related deaths and admissions daily by 10:00 through the State Disaster Management Authority portal.

## Funding

An amount of Rs 48 crore has been allocated under the State Disaster Response Fund for cooling centres, water points and ORS procurement. Districts shall submit utilisation plans by 30 November 2026.

## Compliance

Heads of Department will be held personally responsible for compliance. The Chief Secretary will review progress with all District Collectors on the first Monday of every month from November 2026.

This issues with the approval of the Chief Minister.

Chief Secretary, Government of Vindhara.
