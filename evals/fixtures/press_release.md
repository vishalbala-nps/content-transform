# Lumora Health launches NurseAssist, the world's most advanced AI co-pilot for nurses

FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE. Bengaluru, 10 September 2026.

Lumora Health today announced the launch of NurseAssist, a revolutionary AI co-pilot that transforms the way nurses work. NurseAssist listens to bedside conversations, drafts nursing notes and flags missed medication rounds, giving nurses back the time they need to do what they do best: care.

In an eight-week pilot with 120 nurses at two partner hospitals, NurseAssist cut the time nurses spent on documentation by up to 40%. Nurses in the pilot rated it 4.7 out of 5.

Lumora also announced that it has raised $18 million in a Series A round led by Halcyon Ventures, with participation from existing investors. The funds will be used to expand the engineering team and bring NurseAssist to 50 hospitals by the end of 2027.

"This is the most important thing to happen to nursing in a generation," said Ritu Bhandari, co-founder and CEO of Lumora Health. "Nurses are drowning in paperwork. NurseAssist throws them a lifeline, and we are only getting started."

"Lumora is building the future of hospital care," said Arjun Patil, partner at Halcyon Ventures. "We have never seen a clinical AI product win over staff this fast."

NurseAssist will be available to hospitals from 1 November 2026 at Rs 1,200 per nurse per month. All patient data is processed within the country and is never used to train models.

## About Lumora Health

Lumora Health builds AI tools for hospital staff. Founded in 2023, the company is headquartered in Bengaluru and has 65 employees.
